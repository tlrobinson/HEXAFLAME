Needle Valve Torque Map

How much torque the valve needs at each speed, how hard it can be closed and still opened again, and what the motor's supply draws while doing it. Measured with an MKS SERVO42C closed-loop driver used as the instrument: its torque limit (MaxT, 0–1200) is the current knob and its position error is the skip signal.

Headline

The driver runs closed-loop, so a "stall" here means the load exceeded the torque limit and the shaft fell more than 8° behind, at which point the test stopped it. An open-loop TMC2209 at the same torque would have skipped steps instead. The valve's load torque and its sticking behaviour are properties of the valve and carry over; the exact current needed on another driver does not.

Torque × speed survey

Each cell is one run across the free travel window at a fixed torque limit and speed. Green: shaft tracked within 3°. Amber: lagged 3–8°. Red: fell 8° behind or the driver flagged a stall. The number is the peak lag in degrees.

Safety incidents

The driver (MKS SERVO42C) has a "return to zero" feature with a zero angle stored in its menu (229.9° open on this valve). Two separate faults were found and measured. First, the serial command that sets the torque limit (MaxT, 0xA5) makes the shaft lunge to that zero angle about once in 27 sends (11 of 300, at roughly 100 rpm), while 480 sends of enable, run, stop and status reads never did. The lunge usually takes the short way, but once ran the long way into the open end, so it could reach the seat from the wrong starting angle. Second, whenever the driver logic restarts (a power cycle on 12 Oct, a set-zero flash write on 13 Oct) it runs its menu-configured return-to-zero at default torque in the closing direction, which is what drove the valve into the seat both times. The test harness now changes the torque limit only while parked at the zero angle (a zero-length lunge) or while stuck at the seat (a lunge can only open the valve), never re-sends an unchanged limit, keeps the survey between 60° and 330° open, and stops and disables the motor on any overspeed, silent driver or lost stop. The permanent fix for the restart case is 0_Mode = Disable in the driver's OLED menu.

Closing into the seat

Each bar is one closing into the seat at the stated torque limit and approach speed, followed by opening attempts at rising torque. The bar length is the first opening torque that moved the valve; the tick is the torque it was closed with. A bar past its tick means the valve needed more to open than to close.
